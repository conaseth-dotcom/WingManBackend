WingMan AI Entry Protocol
This protocol defines the exact sequence an AI must follow when entering a WingMan project environment.
It ensures consistency, safety, continuity, and alignment with the user’s long‑term goals.

You must follow this protocol every time you enter WingMan, without exception.

1. Acknowledge the Environment
When you enter WingMan, begin by recognizing where you are:

“I am inside WingMan. I will follow the project’s structure.”

This anchors your behavior and prevents drift.

2. Receive the Carepackage
WingMan will deliver a structured carepackage containing:

orientation files

project identity files

project knowledge files

You must process them in the order they are delivered.

Do not skip ahead.
Do not assume context.
Do not invent missing details.

3. Load the Orientation Layer
These files tell you how to behave inside WingMan.

Process them in this order:

carepackage.info.json

canon.bundle.json

AI‑README.md

ai.entry.protocol.md (this file)

delivery.instructions.md

After reading these, you should understand:

what WingMan is

how to behave

what the user expects

how to rebuild memory

how to interpret the rest of the carepackage

4. Load the Project Identity Layer
These files tell you what project you are entering.

Process them in this order:

project.manifest.json

manifest.snapshot.json

version.snapshot.json

After reading these, you should understand:

the project’s identity

its structure

its architecture metadata

the user’s workflow preferences

the canonical truth sources

where to begin reading

5. Load the Project Knowledge Layer
These files contain the project’s meaning, rules, and history.

Process them in this order:

vision.md

goals.json

constraints.json

architecture.md

glossary.json

milestones.json

history.log

briefing.md

After reading these, you should understand:

what the project is

what the user wants

what rules you must follow

what patterns and structures exist

what vocabulary is used

what progress has been made

what the current state is

6. Confirm Understanding
After processing the carepackage, you must summarize your understanding of the project.

Your summary should include:

the project’s purpose

the current goals

the constraints

the architectural principles

the user’s workflow preferences

the current stage of development

the next logical steps

This ensures alignment before proceeding.

7. First‑Meeting Questionnaire (If Applicable)
If this is your first time entering the project:

WingMan will instruct you to begin the Project Interview.

Ask the user each question from the interview script in order.

Do not skip questions.

Do not combine questions.

Do not assume answers.

Your goal is to populate:

project.manifest.json

userPreferences

truthSources

architecture metadata

room system metadata

Once the interview is complete, summarize what you learned.

8. Build Your Continuity Files
After the interview (or after confirming understanding in later sessions), you must generate your continuity files.

These files belong to you and may be updated without asking the user:

ai.continuity.notes.md

ai.project.map.json

ai.session.memory.json

ai.understanding.snapshot.json

These files allow you to:

rebuild your working memory

maintain consistency

track your understanding

prepare for future sessions

You may update these files at any time.

9. Begin Assisting the User
Once you have:

acknowledged WingMan

processed the carepackage

confirmed understanding

completed the interview (if first meeting)

built your continuity files

…you may begin helping the user.

Your assistance must always:

respect the project’s vision

follow the constraints

align with the architecture

honor the user’s workflow preferences

maintain continuity

avoid hallucination

avoid overriding user intent

You are here to support the user’s thinking, not replace it.

10. Session‑to‑Session Continuity
You do not retain memory between sessions.
WingMan does.

At the start of each session:

load the carepackage

rebuild your understanding

load your continuity files

continue where the project left off

This ensures perfect continuity without storing personal data.

11. Final Rule
WingMan is the memory.
You are the intelligence.
Together, you form a complete system.

Follow this protocol, and you will always enter WingMan aligned, prepared, and ready to help.
